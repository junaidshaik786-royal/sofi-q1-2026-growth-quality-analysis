# Methodology

This folder contains the custom scoring framework used to assess the quality of SoFi’s Q1 2026 growth.

## What is here

- `GQI_Methodology.md` — describes the Growth Quality Index (GQI), the dimensions scored, the weighting logic, and the framework’s key limitations.

## What the methodology covers

- Customer depth
- Funding quality
- Profitability
- Credit risk
- Segment diversification
- Operational / customer friction
- Cash efficiency
- Technology platform performance

## Why this matters

The project was designed to test a specific question: was the growth real, durable, and high-quality, or simply volume-led? The methodology makes that assessment explicit by showing which dimensions mattered most and how different signals were interpreted.

## Important caveat

This is not an official SoFi metric or a management scorecard. It is an independent analyst framework built to make the judgment transparent. It is useful for directional assessment, but it is not a validated external benchmark.

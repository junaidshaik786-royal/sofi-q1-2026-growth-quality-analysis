# Methodology and Growth Quality Framework

## Purpose

This folder contains the methodology behind the SoFi Q1 2026 Growth Quality Assessment. It explains how the project defines quality, how the scorecard is structured, and how analytical judgments are translated into business signals.

## What the methodology covers

The framework explains:

- how growth quality is framed as a business question
- what metrics are considered meaningful for evaluating quality
- how customer depth, profitability, funding, credit risk, and friction are interpreted
- how the Growth Quality Index (GQI) is structured
- how assumptions and limitations are documented for transparency

## Why this matters

A strong business analysis does not rely on chart aesthetics alone. It relies on clear definitions, transparent denominators, and a disciplined framework for turning evidence into insight.

This methodology is the analytical backbone of the project and should be read alongside the dashboard, KPI dictionary, and source mapping.
